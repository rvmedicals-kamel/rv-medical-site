The Story

Where is the opportunity lying?

Turkey's healthcare infrastructure has undergone a remarkable transformation over the past two decades. New large-scale hospital infrastructures and the restructuring of older facilities have released substantial quantities of medical equipment into the market.

Hospitals have closed, relocated, restructured and consolidated. Equipment that served healthcare for years became available — much of it from recognized international manufacturers, much of it with years of serviceable life remaining.

"For some, it was simply the end of a hospital chapter. For us, it became the beginning of a new opportunity."

RV Medical's technical team assesses, prepares and makes this equipment available to healthcare buyers internationally — with the transparency that serious buyers demand.
